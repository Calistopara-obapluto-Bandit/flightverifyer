02 Method

How a record is verified

  1. 01

    Normalise the reference

    Spaces and dashes are stripped, then the reference is matched against booking IDs, e-ticket numbers and PNRs in the ledger.

  2. 02

    Reconcile the reservation

    The record is checked against the carrier reservation entry: ticket status, itinerary sectors, cabin, fare basis and issuing office.

  3. 03

    Attach the manifest

    Passengers, seats, baggage allowance and special service requests are attached to the booking so the dossier is complete.

  4. 04

    Read the aircraft

    For flights in the air, the aircraft's ADS-B transponder supplies position, altitude, speed and heading for the telemetry panel and radar console.

Verification confirms what the reservation ledger holds. Boarding still requires the airline's own documents and a valid travel document. Check gates and times with the carrier at least 24 hours before departure.
